What is RIP? 🤔

🛡️💰🔐

Remorse Insurance Protocol

RIP is a Zero-Knowledge Price Protection System that lets you insure any product purchase without revealing what you bought, how much you paid, or any personal details.

  • 🛍️ Shop anywhere: Buy products on Amazon, eBay, or any e-commerce site
  • 🔐 Commit privately: Generate cryptographic commitment of your purchase
  • 💳 Buy protection: Pay a small premium based on price tier, not product type
  • 📊 Automatic monitoring: Oracle tracks prices using Ethereum blobs
  • ⚡ Instant claims: Prove price drops with ZK proofs, get paid automatically
